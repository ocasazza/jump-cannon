# Workspace

The Graph panel is the primary surface. Nodes, Inspector, Document, Progress,
and Settings support repeated exploration without leaving the workspace.

Use the Graph header's Fit action whenever the graph leaves the viewport. Open
notes through [[Nodes Search and Documents]], then use [[Layouts Metrics and Filters]]
to compare structure. Panel placement persists locally through panel-kit.
