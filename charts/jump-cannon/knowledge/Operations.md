# Operations

Package the application through [[Helm Deployment]] and promote it through
[[GitOps Release]]. Expose stable names through [[Service Access]] and
[[NetBird Access]], then verify behavior through [[Observability]].

Recurring quality gates live in [[Scheduled Tests]]. GPU work follows
[[Compute]]. Apply [[Security Model]] throughout and start incident diagnosis in
[[Troubleshooting]].
